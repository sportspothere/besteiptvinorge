## Owner confirmations still needed

- Rights and permissions for website text, illustrations, images, posters, logos
  and other original/third-party assets; no comprehensive ownership was asserted.
- Actual licensing/distribution authorizations for TV and on-demand content in
  relevant territories; the page states the requirement without asserting licences.
- Actual content-supply, hosting and control relationships; no technical-intermediary
  status or blanket absence of responsibility is claimed.
- Complaint-handling ownership, verification and escalation process, provider contacts,
  and realistic operational response times. The policy promises no invented deadline.
- Whether US DMCA applies to any activity and whether a designated agent has been
  appointed and registered where required. No agent details or automatic safe-harbor
  status were invented. The ordinary support channel is not presented as a registered
  DMCA agent or a substitute for formal requirements in cases where they apply.
- Complaint-data retention, recipients, disclosure criteria and safeguards should be
  included in the broader outstanding privacy review. The page minimizes requested
  information and links to the existing privacy policy instead of inventing practices.
